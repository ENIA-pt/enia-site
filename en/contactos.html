Who comes to us

  • Companies using AI without knowing what risk tier they are in

    The most common case. Half an hour is usually enough to establish whether Annex III applies.

  • Suppliers whose customer has asked for proof of compliance

    Usually with a deadline already running and no baseline documentation.

  • Public bodies preparing to procure AI solutions

    Contract clauses, transparency requirements and deployer responsibilities.

  • Universities and research centres

    Applied research, sandboxes and liaison with the Universities and Research College.
