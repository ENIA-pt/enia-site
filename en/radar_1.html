          What companies do with AI

              Why the numbers diverge — and what we do about it

              Two readings of business AI adoption in Portugal are in circulation. The European Commission’s DESI puts it at around 17%, twice the European average. INE, in its 2025 IUTICE survey, finds 11.5%.

              This is not a contradiction, it is scope: the two series use different definitions and different universes — INE covers only companies with ten or more employees, and the European question on AI technologies was reworded between editions. What both show, beyond any doubt, is the same direction of travel: adoption is rising, it is deeply uneven by company size, and it is far ahead of the country’s capacity to govern it.

              We publish both, each with its source attached. A radar that picks the more flattering number stops being a radar.
